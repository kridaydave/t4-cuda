Hypothesis H7: Sub-Byte INT3 Dequantization Confirmed

Unpacks non-byte aligned signed 3-bit integers ($s3 \in [-4, 3]$) in 1 SASS cycle using lop3.b32 LUT 0xCA and magic constant 0x64046404.

// Single-cycle LOP3 INT3 Unpack (LUT 0xCA)
asm volatile("lop3.b32 %0, %1, %2, %3, 0xCA;" 
  : "=r"(r01) 
  : "r"(packed_w), "r"(0x00070007), "r"(0x64046404));
w01 = __hfma2(reinterpret_cast<half2&>(r01), scale, neg_bias);
